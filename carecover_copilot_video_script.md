## Scene-by-Scene Script & Voiceover

### 🎬 Scene 1: Introduction & Problem Statement (0:00 - 0:45)
**Visual Cue:** Screen shows the CareCover Copilot Executive Dashboard with clean glassmorphism UI, displaying active policy health scores and sum insured breakdown.

🎙️ **Voiceover (Presenter):**
> "When a medical emergency strikes, patients and caregivers face immense stress. In India, health insurance policies are filled with complex legalese—room rent caps, sub-limits, proportional deductions, and pre-authorization notice rules that often result in massive, unexpected out-of-pocket bills at hospital discharge.
>
> Welcome to **CareCover Copilot**—an independent, enterprise-grade AI navigation system designed to empower patients, caregivers, and policyholders with instant clarity, financial risk prediction, and seamless hospital navigation before taking medical steps."

---

### 🎬 Scene 2: Dual-Policy RAG Engine & DPDP 2025 Privacy (0:45 - 1:45)
**Visual Cue:** Presenter selects a sample policy (Niva Bupa / Star Comprehensive) or uploads a PDF. The dashboard updates in real-time to display extracted Sum Insured, Room Category Eligibility, Co-Pay, and Exclusion clauses. Presenter clicks the "Purge Data" button to demonstrate DPDP 2025 consent receipt generation.

🎙️ **Voiceover (Presenter):**
> "At the heart of CareCover Copilot is a Dual-Policy Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Engine. Users can upload their Primary Base Policy and a secondary Super Top-Up policy schedule. The system instantly parses complex contracts using PyMuPDF and ChromaDB, extracting critical parameters like room rent limits, Cataract sub-limits, and deductible trigger thresholds.
>
> What about privacy? CareCover is strictly **DPDP Rules 2025 compliant**. Policy documents are processed purely in ephemeral RAM—never stored on disk. With one click on 'Purge Data', all session memory is completely wiped, generating an auditable Cryptographic Deletion Certificate."

---

### 🎬 Scene 3: Cashless Hospital Locator & Financial Risk Calculator (1:45 - 2:45)
**Visual Cue:** Screen switches to the *Hospitals & Cost* tab. User types 'Pune' or 'Bengaluru' and selects 'Cardiology'. Results show cashless network status, distance in km, match score, and room rate per night. Then, presenter moves to the *Financial Risk Calculator*, entering a hospital bill amount to show out-of-pocket breakdown.

🎙️ **Voiceover (Presenter):**
> "Navigating to hospital care is seamless. Our *Cashless Hospital Locator* indexes network hospitals across India, scoring each facility by specialty, cashless status, and live distance.
>
> Before checking into a room, users can run our *Financial Risk & Penalty Engine*. If a patient selects a Deluxe Suite when their policy only covers a Single Private Room, our engine automatically calculates the 15% to 25% proportional penalty applied across doctor fees and surgery charges, giving an exact forecast of expected out-of-pocket expenses before admission."

---

### 🎬 Scene 4: Explainable AI Audit & 23 Indian Languages (2:45 - 3:45)
**Visual Cue:** Presenter clicks 'Ask AI' and types: *"Is cataract surgery covered under my policy?"* The AI responds with exact clause citations. Presenter clicks *View Audit Trace* to open the XAI modal (Document Clause → Policy Rule → Verdict). Presenter then changes the language dropdown from English to Hindi / Marathi.

🎙️ **Voiceover (Presenter):**
> "Trust and transparency are mandatory in healthcare AI. CareCover Copilot features an **Explainable AI (XAI) Traceability Audit Chain**. Every answer provided by our AI assistant comes with verifiable evidence line items, mapping the conclusion directly back to the exact policy clause.
>
> To ensure accessibility for every Indian citizen, our system integrates the **Bhashini Translation API**, supporting **23 official Indian languages**—including Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, and Gujarati."

---

### 🎬 Scene 5: Emergency SOS & 6-Stage Care Journey (3:45 - 4:30)
**Visual Cue:** Presenter toggles the *Emergency SOS* tab. The header displays dynamic, city-aware emergency numbers (112 National SOS, 108 Ambulance). Screen then switches to the *Claims & Journey* tab showing the 6-stage care timeline (Diagnosis → Pre-Auth → Admission → Treatment → Discharge → Settlement).

🎙️ **Voiceover (Presenter):**
> "In critical emergencies, every second counts. The top Emergency SOS bar automatically detects the user's location, displaying instant 24/7 national emergency lines (112) and state ambulance dispatch numbers (108).
>
> Furthermore, CareCover provides a guided 6-Stage Patient Care Journey—giving step-by-step checklists for cashless pre-authorization, TPA intimation SLAs (48 hours for planned, 24 hours for emergency), and automated document checklists for reimbursement claims."

---

### 🎬 Scene 6: Tech Stack, Live URL & Conclusion (4:30 - 5:00)
**Visual Cue:** Screen transitions to the live application URL ([carecover-copilot-production.up.railway.app](https://carecover-copilot-production.up.railway.app/)) and displays the tech stack summary slide.

🎙️ **Voiceover (Presenter):**
> "Built with a Python FastAPI backend, Tailwind CSS, ChromaDB vector storage, and Groq Llama-3.3 intelligence, CareCover Copilot is fully containerized with Docker and live on Railway today.
>
> CareCover Copilot bridges the gap between complex insurance policies and real-world patient care—saving families from financial surprises when they need support the most.
>
> Thank you! Explore the live platform at **carecover-copilot-production.up.railway.app**."
